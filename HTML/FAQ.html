Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shopping with Lord Look.

1. What is Lord Look?
Lord Look is a fashion brand focused on premium-quality apparel and accessories designed to combine comfort, style, and everyday wear.
2. How can I place an order?
Simply browse our collection, select your preferred size and quantity, add the product to your cart, and complete the checkout process.
3. What payment methods do you accept?
We accept secure online payments through UPI and debit card. Available payment options will be displayed during checkout.
4. Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?
No. We currently do not offer Cash on Delivery (COD). All orders must be prepaid using one of our secure online payment methods, including UPI, credit cards, debit cards, net banking, or other available payment options displayed during checkout.
5. How long does shipping take?
Orders are typically processed within 1–3 business days and delivered within 2–8 business days, depending on your location.
6. How can I track my order?
Once your order is shipped, you'll receive a tracking number via email or WhatsApp (where applicable) to monitor your shipment.
7. Can I cancel my order?
Orders can only be cancelled before they are shipped. Once dispatched, cancellations are no longer possible.
8. Can I return or exchange my order?
Yes. Eligible products can be returned or exchanged according to our Return & Refund Policy. Items must be unused, unwashed, and returned in their original condition with all tags attached within 2 days of delivery.
9. What if I receive a damaged or incorrect item?
Please contact us within 48 hours of receiving your order. Include your order number along with clear photos of the product and packaging, and we'll help resolve the issue promptly.
10. How do I choose the right size?
Each product page includes a detailed size guide to help you select the best fit. If you're between sizes, we generally recommend choosing the smaller size for a more comfortable fit.
11. Will the product color match the photos?
We strive to display our products as accurately as possible. However, slight color variations may occur due to differences in screen settings and lighting.
12. How should I care for my garments?
  • Wash inside out with similar colors.
  • Use cold water and a mild detergent.
  • Do not bleach.
  • Avoid direct ironing on printed designs.
  • Air dry whenever possible.
13. Do you restock sold-out products?
Some popular products may be restocked, while limited-edition items may not return. Follow us on social media or subscribe to our updates for restock announcements.
14. Do you ship internationally?
Yes. Lord Look ships within India and internationally.

International shipping rates and estimated delivery times vary depending on the destination country. Shipping charges and available delivery options will be calculated and displayed during checkout.

Please note that international customers are responsible for any applicable customs duties, import taxes, or other fees imposed by their country's customs authorities. No return or exchange policy applies for international shipments.

16. Is my payment secure?
Yes. All payments are processed through secure payment gateways using industry-standard encryption. Lord Look does not store your complete payment information.
17. Can I modify my shipping address after placing an order?
Address changes may be possible only before your order has been shipped. Please contact us as soon as possible after placing your order.
18. What happens if my package is returned to Lord Look?
If a package is returned due to an incorrect address or failed delivery attempts, we'll contact you to arrange re-shipment. Additional shipping charges may apply.
19. Are your products authentic?
Yes. Every product sold by Lord Look is an original product designed or sourced by our brand and undergoes quality checks before dispatch.
20. How can I stay updated on new collections and offers?
Subscribe to our newsletter and follow Lord Look on our official social media channels to receive updates on new arrivals, exclusive collections, and special offers.
