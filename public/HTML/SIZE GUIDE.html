How to Measure

Chest

Measure across the garment, 1 inch below the armhole from one side to the other.

Length

Measure from the highest point of the shoulder down to the bottom hem.

Shoulder

Measure from shoulder seam to shoulder seam across the back.

Sleeve

Measure from the shoulder seam to the sleeve opening.
